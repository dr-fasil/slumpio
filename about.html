Why slump.IO?

Concrete mix design is a critical engineering task — but most tools are either desktop-only, require expensive licenses, or don't follow the correct standards rigorously.

slump.IO was built to change that. It runs entirely on your iPhone, works offline, follows the standards precisely, and produces professional PDF reports you can share directly from the field.

Whether you're a site engineer checking a batch, a researcher designing an HPC trial mix, or a student learning mix design methodology — slump.IO gives you the tools you need.

Supported Standards

All calculations are implemented directly from the original standard documents.

ACI 211.1
Standard Practice for Selecting Proportions for Normal, Heavyweight, and Mass Concrete — American Concrete Institute
IS 10262:2019
Concrete Mix Proportioning — Guidelines (Second Revision) — Bureau of Indian Standards
BS 8500
Concrete — Complementary British Standard to BS EN 206 — British Standards Institution
