Features

Everything you need on site

Designed for engineers who need accurate, fast results — in the field or in the lab.

🧮

Three Design Standards

Full implementation of ACI 211.1, IS 10262:2019, and BS 8500. Switch standards instantly. All calculations validated against the original standards.

⚗️

Custom Mix Mode

Specify total binder content and W/B ratio directly. Perfect for UHPC, research trials, and proprietary mix designs. Full volume check included.

🔬

Advanced SCM Support

Dual supplementary cementitious materials — Fly Ash, GGBS, Silica Fume. ACI k-value efficiency factors applied automatically for W/CM calculation.

🌡️

Field Corrections

Moisture and absorption corrections for coarse and fine aggregates. Temperature warnings for hot and cold weather concreting per ACI 305R and 306R.

📄

Professional PDF Reports

Generate and share complete mix design reports with your name, company, and project details. Includes pie chart, batch quantities, and field-adjusted values.

📦

Mix Archive

Save unlimited designs. Search by name or notes. Separate sections for standard and custom designs. Edit and recalculate any saved design.

🌐

Metric & Imperial

Switch between Metric (MPa, mm, kg/m³) and Imperial (psi, in, °F) instantly. All validation messages and outputs update automatically.

🎓

Field Test Guides

Built-in reference guides for slump test (ASTM C143), air content (ASTM C231), fresh density (ASTM C138), and temperature (ASTM C1064) with YouTube tutorials.

🔒

Fully Offline & Private

All calculations and data stored locally on your device. No account required. No data ever leaves your phone. Works anywhere, even without internet.
