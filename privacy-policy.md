# Privacy Policy

**slump.IO** — Concrete Mix Design  
Last updated: May 2026

## Overview

slump.IO is a concrete mix design tool for engineers and construction professionals. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

## Data We Collect

**slump.IO does not collect any personal data.**

All mix designs, calculations, and settings are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard storage (UserDefaults and local file storage). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

## Data We Do Not Collect

- We do not collect your name, email, or contact information
- We do not collect location data
- We do not collect device identifiers or analytics
- We do not use third-party tracking or advertising SDKs
- We do not share any data with third parties

## Feedback

If you choose to send feedback using the in-app feedback form, your message is submitted to our feedback service (web3forms.com). This submission includes only the message text and the name you optionally enter in your profile. No other data is transmitted.

## Profile Information

If you enter your name and company in the Profile section, this information is stored locally on your device only and is used solely to populate PDF reports you generate. It is never transmitted or shared.

## PDF Reports

PDF reports are generated entirely on your device. When you choose to share a report, it is shared directly from your device using iOS standard sharing. We do not receive or store any copy of your reports.

## Children's Privacy

slump.IO is a professional engineering tool not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
