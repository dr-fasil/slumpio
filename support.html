Frequently Asked Questions

Which design standards does slump.IO support?

slump.IO supports ACI 211.1 (USA), IS 10262:2019 (India), BS 8500 (UK), and a Custom mix mode for direct binder-based design. All calculations follow the original standard documents.

What units does the app support?

Both Metric (MPa, mm, kg/m³) and Imperial (psi, inches, °F). Switch between them using the toggle in the Compose tab or Settings tab. All validation messages and outputs update automatically.

How do I save a mix design?

After calculating, tap Save on the results screen. Give it a name or leave it blank for auto-naming. All saved designs appear in the Archive tab, organized into Standard and Custom sections.

Can I export my results?

Yes. Open any saved design from the Archive, tap Preview PDF to view the full report, then Export PDF to share via email, AirDrop, WhatsApp, or any other app.

Does the app work offline?

Yes, completely. All calculations run on-device. Your data is stored locally. The app never requires an internet connection.

Can I edit a saved design?

Yes. Open any design from the Archive and tap Edit & Recalculate to modify inputs and recalculate with the updated values.

What is Custom mix mode?

Custom mode lets you specify total binder content and W/B ratio directly, without going through a standard design method. This is useful for UHPC, proprietary mix designs, and research trials. It includes a full volume check to verify your inputs sum to 1.0 m³.

What SCMs are supported?

Fly Ash, GGBS (Ground Granulated Blast-Furnace Slag), and Silica Fume. You can use up to two SCMs simultaneously (ternary blend). ACI k-values (efficiency factors) are applied automatically when using ACI 211.1.

What are the Field Test Guides?

Built-in reference guides for on-site testing: Slump Test (ASTM C143 / BS 1881-102), Air Content by Pressure Method (ASTM C231), Fresh Density (ASTM C138), and Concrete Temperature (ASTM C1064). Each links to a YouTube tutorial.

Is my data backed up?

Currently, data is stored locally on your device. We recommend using iOS device backups (iCloud or iTunes) to protect your data. iCloud sync between devices is planned for a future release.
