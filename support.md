## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which design standards does slump.IO support?**  
slump.IO supports ACI 211.1 (USA), IS 10262:2019 (India), BS 8500 (UK), and a Custom mix mode for direct binder-based design.

**What units does the app support?**  
Both Metric (MPa, mm, kg/m³) and Imperial (psi, inches, lb/ft³). Switch between them in the Compose tab or Settings.

**How do I save a mix design?**  
After calculating, tap **Save** on the results screen. All saved designs appear in the Archive tab.

**Can I export my results?**  
Yes. Open any saved design from the Archive, tap **Preview PDF** to view, then **Export PDF** to share via email, AirDrop, or any other app.

**Where is my data stored?**  
All data is stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

**Can I edit a saved design?**  
Yes. Open the design from Archive and tap **Edit & Recalculate**.

**What is Custom mix mode?**  
Custom mode lets you specify total binder content and W/B ratio directly, without going through a standard design method. Useful when you already know your mix proportions.
