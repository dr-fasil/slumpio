Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

✅ The short version: slump.IO does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Overview

slump.IO is a concrete mix design tool for engineers and construction professionals. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data We Collect

slump.IO does not collect any personal data.

All mix designs, calculations, and settings are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard storage (UserDefaults and local file storage). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or contact information
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not collect device identifiers or analytics
  • We do not use third-party tracking or advertising SDKs
  • We do not share any data with third parties
  • We do not use cookies or web tracking

Feedback

If you choose to send feedback using the in-app feedback form, your message is submitted to our feedback service (web3forms.com). This submission includes only the message text and the name you optionally enter in your profile. No other data is transmitted. Submitting feedback is entirely voluntary.

Profile Information

If you enter your name and company in the Profile section, this information is stored locally on your device only and is used solely to populate PDF reports you generate. It is never transmitted or shared with anyone.

PDF Reports

PDF reports are generated entirely on your device. When you choose to share a report, it is shared directly from your device using iOS standard sharing features. We do not receive or store any copy of your reports.

Children's Privacy

slump.IO is a professional engineering tool not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used is web3forms.com, which powers the optional in-app feedback form. This service only receives the message text you type and the optional name from your profile. No other data is shared.

Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, it is protected by your device's own security features (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID). We recommend keeping your iOS device updated and using device backup (iCloud or iTunes) to protect your data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or applicable law. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
