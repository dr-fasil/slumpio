Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

The Short Version

  • No data is collected or transmitted — everything stays on your device.
  • No account or login is required.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
  • Feedback you submit voluntarily goes only to the developer.
  • The app works fully offline.

1. Information We Collect

slump.IO does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All mix designs, profile information, and settings are stored locally on your device using iOS data storage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly share a PDF report.

2. Feedback Submissions

If you choose to submit feedback through the in-app feedback form, your message and the name you have entered in your profile are sent to the developer via Web3Forms. No other data is transmitted. This submission is entirely voluntary.

3. Analytics & Tracking

slump.IO contains no analytics, tracking SDKs, crash reporters, or advertising networks. No usage data of any kind is collected.

4. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used is Web3Forms, which processes voluntary feedback submissions. No other third-party services, SDKs, or APIs are integrated into the app.

5. Children's Privacy

slump.IO does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app is intended for professional and academic use by adults.

6. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available at this URL. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the current policy.
